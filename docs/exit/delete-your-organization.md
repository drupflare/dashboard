Deleting an organization is not available yet. Nothing in the dashboard closes an organization.

You can delete individual sites. A deleted site is kept for 90 days before its records are removed. See [Delete a site](/docs/guides/sites/delete-and-restore).

Before you delete sites, export what you need:

1. Export each site's backups. See [Export your site](/docs/guides/sites/export-your-site).
2. Export the cost-center report as CSV.
3. Remove site grants and delegations (the access you gave an agency to work on a site), so no one else keeps working on a site you delete.

Ask Drupflare how an organization closure is handled. Until a closure route exists, a closure is a manual request.
