This page says that logs and metrics are not available yet. It is for owners looking for a site's logs or charts.

Logs and metrics are not available in the dashboard yet. The site pages have no log view and no metrics chart.

These views show what the dashboard knows about a site today.

- The [health tree](/docs/guides/monitoring/health-tree) shows the checks the platform ran on the last read.
- The [automatic repairs](/docs/guides/monitoring/automatic-repairs) list shows each repair, what was seen before and after it, and its status.
- **Recent Activity** on the site overview lists changes to the site.
