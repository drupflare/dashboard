This page says that incidents are not available yet. It is for owners who look for incident records.

Incidents are not available yet. The dashboard has no incident records, so nothing opens or closes as an incident.

To see what went wrong and what the platform did, use these two views.

- The [health tree](/docs/guides/monitoring/health-tree) shows what is wrong now.
- **Recent Activity** on the site overview lists changes to the site. It shows No Activity Yet when the list is empty.
- The [automatic repairs](/docs/guides/monitoring/automatic-repairs) list shows each repair the platform made and what it found.
