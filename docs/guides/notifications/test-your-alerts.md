You can test mail to an organization contact. The test checks that the address works and that the contact is set to receive operational mail.

## Send a test

Open Contacts for the organization. Admins and owners can see it. Find the contact's role and choose Send a Test Notification.

A test sends one email to the contact. The email says it is a test, names the organization and the role, and needs no action.

## When a test does not send

- The contact is not confirmed. Choose Send Confirmation, follow the link in the email, then try again. The link lasts 7 days.
- The contact's notifications switch is off. The page says so, and nothing is sent. Turn the switch on, save, then test again.
- The mail service did not accept the message. The page says to try again later.

The test is optional operational mail, and it has no unsubscribe link yet. See [what we email](/docs/guides/notifications/what-we-email).
