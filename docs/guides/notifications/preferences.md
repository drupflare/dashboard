You choose which optional emails you get. Open Notifications in your account settings to see the list. Optional emails start on, so you get them until you switch them off.

## What you can switch

- **Operations Updates.** Findings on your sites, results of upgrades, restores and repairs, and access reviews that open on their schedule. You get one email for each event. Owners and admins get the access review emails.
- **Billing Information.** Invoice copies and usage notices. This includes a note when a site passes 75, 90, 100 or 250 percent of its monthly view allowance. You get at most one email for each level a month. Owners and billing members get these.
- **Reports.** Client and site reports. No report email exists yet, so this switch has no effect for now.
- **Daily Summary.** One email a day in place of one email per event. This is not available yet. The page shows it, and it does not save a choice.

## What you cannot switch off

Some emails always send. They have no switch, because you would be worse off without them.

- Sign-in and recovery codes. You need them to get into your account.
- Invitations. The person invited needs the email to join.
- Security events, such as a new sign-in method.
- Failed payments.
- Stop alerts. A site at a hard budget stops serving visitors. The warning at 90 percent of the budget comes first. Both always reach you.
- Changes to your agreement, such as a price or terms change.

## Stop one kind from the email

Each optional email has a link at the bottom. Open it and confirm. That turns off the one kind of email that sent the message and nothing else. You can use the link more than once, and it does no harm. Your email app may also show an unsubscribe button. It does the same thing.

You can turn the email back on from Notifications in your account settings.

## Organization contacts

A contact on your organization is not a person with an account. It has its own notify switch on the contacts page. The switch is its choice for billing information mail. The unsubscribe link in that mail turns the switch off.

## Pausing

Drupflare can pause all optional mail across the platform. The pause stops optional emails only. Essential emails still send.
