This page says what to expect after a cutover, which is not available yet. It is for owners planning a move.

Moves are not available yet. A site's move page shows Moves Are Not Available Yet, and the move route answers 501 until moves are available. There is no cutover (the switch of your address to the new site) to check yet.

A move never deletes the old site. It stays in place with its data, whatever the move does.
