This page shows you which provider accounts you can sign in with. It is for everyone who signs in to Drupflare.

The Connected Accounts section lists the provider accounts you can sign in with: GitHub, GitLab, Cloudflare, Google and Microsoft.

Connecting an account lets you sign in with it. A connection gives Drupflare no access to your repositories or Cloudflare resources. Each connection shows the email it uses and the date you connected it. Choose Remove to delete a connection.
