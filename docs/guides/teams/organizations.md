This page explains what an organization holds. It is for every owner and admin.

An organization is the top-level account in Drupflare. It holds:

- **People.** Each person is a member with one organization role. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/guides/teams/roles-and-permissions).
- **Sites.** Every site belongs to one organization. A site can also be placed in a department inside it.
- **Billing.** The plan, invoices and payment method belong to the organization. See [Subscribe and invoices](/docs/guides/billing/subscribe-and-invoices).
- **Clients and delegations.** Agencies group client sites under client workspaces and can receive sites from other organizations. See [Client workspaces](/docs/guides/teams/client-workspaces) and [Delegate to an agency](/docs/guides/teams/delegate-to-an-agency).
- **Sign-in and keys.** Organization sign-in and API keys are set per organization. See [Single sign-on](/docs/guides/teams/sso) and [API keys](/docs/guides/teams/api-keys).

Invitations go out by email. An invitation expires after 7 days, and an admin can resend or revoke it before then. The member list shows each person's role and when they joined.

Departments split an organization into a tree for institutions. Each department has its own members and roles, and a Site Limit can cap the sites in it. See [Department administrators](/docs/institutions/department-administrators).

Deleting an organization is not available yet. Transfers between organizations are covered in [Transfer a site](/docs/guides/teams/transfer-a-site).
