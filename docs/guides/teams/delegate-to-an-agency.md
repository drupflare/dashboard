This page shows you how to let an agency work on your sites while you keep ownership and billing. It is for organization owners.

A delegation lets an agency work on sites that belong to your organization. You keep ownership and billing. The agency gets the site actions the delegation covers.

## How it works

1. An admin in the agency's organization creates a delegation and picks the sites to offer, or picks all sites.
2. An admin in your organization accepts or declines it on the Delegations page at `/org/delegations`.
3. Once accepted, the agency's people can use the delegated actions on those sites.
4. Either side can end the delegation.

A delegation is one batch. Each selected site is a row in the batch, so accepting or ending the batch changes all of its sites at once. Accepting and ending a batch are each one request.

## What the agency can and cannot do

The agency can use the site actions in the delegation. It cannot:

- Transfer a site. See [Transfer a site](/docs/guides/teams/transfer-a-site).
- Change your organization's billing or plan.
- Use a delegation after it has ended.

If the agency's organization is suspended, its delegated access stops at the next request.

For the full handover steps, see [Handoff and transfer](/docs/agencies/handoff-and-transfer).
