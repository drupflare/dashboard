This page explains what happens to your plan and account when a charge is refunded or disputed. It is for owners who ask for a refund.

A refund does not change your plan or your subscription. When a charge is refunded or disputed, the organization's account is flagged for a person to review. The flag is the only change in the dashboard.

The dashboard has no refund request form. Contact [support@drupflare.com](mailto:support@drupflare.com) about a refund.
