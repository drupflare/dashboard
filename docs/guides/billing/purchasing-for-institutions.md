This page shows you where to keep the documents a purchase needs. It is for institution owners and billing members.

The Procurement page keeps a record of the documents your purchase needs: quotes, purchase orders, invoices and contracts. Owners, admins and billing contacts can see it.

## Record a document

Add a record with its kind and the details you enter. The dashboard keeps the amounts as text you typed. It does not add up totals or work out tax.

Change a record when its details change. Records are listed oldest first.

## Export

Download the organization's procurement records as CSV. Text cells that start with a formula character are prefixed with an apostrophe.

## Not covered here

How Drupflare accepts a purchase order, a contract or a consortium agreement is not described in the dashboard. Contact [support@drupflare.com](mailto:support@drupflare.com) for those terms.
