This page lists the plans and what each one includes. It is for owners choosing a plan.

The Billing page lists each plan with what it includes, such as the number of sites and whether the plan can upload your own code.

## Prices

A plan shows its price only after Drupflare has reviewed it. A price that shows as unknown or negotiated has no published figure. Ask Drupflare for the price of a plan that does not show one.

## Starting a subscription

Checkout opens for a managed plan. A plan without a reviewed price cannot be bought.

Save the billing contact, name and country first. Checkout does not open until they are saved.

## Changing a subscription

An organization with a subscription changes it from Manage Billing, not from the plan list. The billing portal opens from there. See [subscribe and invoices](/docs/guides/billing/subscribe-and-invoices).
