This page explains overage, the usage past your plan's views. It is for owners who see overage on their bill.

Overage is usage past the views a plan includes, billed at the overage rate. The rate is not yet reviewed, so this page gives no figure. The budget screen shows "Not billed yet." beside a dollar amount until the rate is reviewed.

## Keep overage under a cap

A spend budget caps counted views as a percent of the included views. A hard budget stops a site once the cap is reached, so overage stops growing there, within the bound described in [spend budgets](/docs/guides/billing/budgets). A soft budget sends alerts and lets usage continue.

For invoices, see [subscribe and invoices](/docs/guides/billing/subscribe-and-invoices). Usage warnings are optional billing mail, and optional mail is not sent yet. See [what we email](/docs/guides/notifications/what-we-email).
