This page shows you what to check before you subscribe to a plan. It is for owners who pay for a plan.

## Before you subscribe

Save the billing contact name, email and country for the organization. The checkout needs them, and it asks for the full address as well.

## Start a subscription

Choose a managed plan. Drupflare opens a hosted checkout page, and you pay there.

Checkout needs a signed-in session. An API key cannot start it.

## Add-ons

An add-on is bought either as a subscription, billed with the plan, or as a one-time pack. On a running subscription, a subscription add-on changes at once. Otherwise a hosted checkout opens.

To change how many of an add-on the subscription holds, set the quantity. A quantity changes only when the payment provider confirms it.

## Invoices

Choose Manage Billing to open the hosted billing portal. The portal is where the organization changes its card, reads its invoices and cancels. Opening it needs a signed-in session.
