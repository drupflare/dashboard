Drupflare does not send invoices to departments. Chargeback (billing departments for their use) is an internal step you run with your own accounts. Showback (a report of what each department uses, without a bill) gives you the numbers to run it.

Use cost centers to group each department's sites. See [Cost centers and showback](/docs/guides/billing/cost-centers-and-showback).

## What the report gives you

- Each cost center's sites and their views for the month.
- Each hour is marked exact, estimated or unaccounted.
- A split of an amount you enter, by each department's share of views.

The Drupflare cost is shown as not measured. The split is only as good as the usage records behind it.

## Current limits

Usage is not collected in production yet. Until it is, the report shows not recorded for every site. Do not use the split for a real chargeback until the usage records show exact or estimated hours.

Drupflare's plan prices are not part of this report. Your contract covers them.
