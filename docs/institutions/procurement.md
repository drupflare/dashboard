Contract terms and prices come from Drupflare directly. This page does not state them.

## Procurement records in the dashboard

An owner or billing user can keep procurement records in the organization: the purchase reference, the amount as typed, and the status. Admins can read them. The amount is stored as text and is not added up.

Records are on the Procurement page at `/org/procurement`. Export them as a CSV (a spreadsheet file). Characters that would start a spreadsheet formula are escaped.

Uploading procurement documents to the dashboard is not available yet.

## Steps

1. Send your questions to Drupflare. Include your security reviewer's list. See [Security review packet](/docs/institutions/security-review-packet).
2. Agree the terms and the price in writing with Drupflare.
3. Record the purchase in the Procurement page, so the reference is on file.
