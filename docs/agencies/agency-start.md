An agency account runs client work in one organization. Each client gets a workspace, and each team member gets the role they need.

Client workspaces and delegations need the agency feature on your plan.

## 1. Set up the organization

Sign in and create the organization. An owner holds the billing and sign-in settings. Keep at least one more owner on the account.

## 2. Add the first client

Open the Clients page at `/org/clients` and add the client by name. See [Client workspaces](/docs/guides/teams/client-workspaces).

## 3. Invite the team

Invite each person by email from the Members page. Pick the role that matches their work. An invitation expires after 7 days. Developers can deploy and change code. Viewers can read but not change. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/guides/teams/roles-and-permissions).

## 4. Connect the client's sites

A client's site can reach your account in one of two ways:

- The client delegates the site to you. See [Delegate to an agency](/docs/guides/teams/delegate-to-an-agency).
- You give one person access to one site. See [Site grants](/docs/guides/teams/site-grants).

Then follow [Client onboarding](/docs/agencies/client-onboarding) for the rest of the steps.
