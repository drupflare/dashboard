A handoff gives the client control of a site. Moving ownership is not available yet.

## What you can do today

1. **Remove your access.** End the delegation for the site, or remove your site grants. Your team then loses its actions on the site. See [Delegate to an agency](/docs/guides/teams/delegate-to-an-agency).
2. **Tell the client what they now hold.** Ownership stays with the client's organization, as it did during the delegation.
3. **Keep a copy of the handoff notes.** Record the site's owners, its restore points and its open repairs.

## What is not available yet

A site transfer to the client's organization. The proposal and acceptance steps work. Finishing the move is not built. See [Transfer a site](/docs/guides/teams/transfer-a-site).

Until the move ships, the client's organization may need to hold the site through a delegation or a grant from you. Do not promise a transfer date.
