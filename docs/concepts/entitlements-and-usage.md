An organization's plan sets what its sites may use. The [plans guide](/docs/guides/billing/plans) lists what each plan includes.

## How usage is recorded

Usage is recorded in hourly intervals for each site. Each interval is marked one of three ways:

- **Exact.** The count is complete for that hour.
- **Estimated.** The count comes from a sample or a partial reading.
- **Unaccounted.** The count could not be read for that hour.
