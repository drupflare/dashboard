Restoring a whole organization is not available yet. There is no way to do it in the dashboard.

Restore one site at a time from its own backups. The site's restore is covered in [Restore a backup](/docs/guides/data/restore-a-backup).

Record which sites you restored and in what order. Tell the organization's owner which sites are back and which are not.
