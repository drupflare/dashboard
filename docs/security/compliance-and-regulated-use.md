Drupflare claims no compliance certification. Not available yet for any standard.

Before you host regulated data (data that a law or a standard governs) on Drupflare, ask for the written position on:

- The specific regulation or standard, such as health, education or payment data.
- Where data is stored. See [Data location](/docs/institutions/data-location).
- The contract terms that cover that data. These come from Drupflare directly.

Do not put regulated data on a site until those answers are in writing.
